\section{Current Evaluation Protocol and Evidence}
\label{app:current-evaluation}
\paragraph{Coverage and acceptance.}
We evaluate all 123 modeled cases with one optimized compiler under
algebraic semantics and a 30-second per-command limit. Acceptance requires
semantic checking, successful compilation, and portable-program validation
under the original guards and supplied fields. The main figure reports
default-backend acceptance; the text separately reports certified acceptance
for inference tasks. The 86 unmodeled catalog cases are excluded from
semantic evaluation and counted as neither successes nor failures.
Exact sources, per-case outcomes, and compiler provenance are retained in
\nolinkurl{evaluation/unified-construction/}.

Across all 123 cases, certified nonacceptance comprises 81 unsupported
operation-template cases, four graph pairs outside the schemas, one
non-read-once structure, and two unresolved guard proofs. No certified
dtype, compilation, loading, or timeout failure occurs. Default outcomes
are unchanged. The original core audit remains 10/38 inference tasks and
12/123 overall. Combined certified coverage increases from 26/38 to 31/38
inference tasks and from 30/123 to 35/123 overall, with no regression.
Ten inference templates use the complete constructor under its domain
assumptions; twenty-one use additional sound schemas. All seven remaining
inference tasks fail both backends and are outside the certified contracts.

The public compile/load/rewrite suite passes 685 independent dense-value
and ordered-shape checks and 142 rejection checks across eight test targets.
It includes ranks 0--4 and 128, zero/singleton extents, unsorted embeddings,
batched dot reversal and scalar extraction, created plus original contracting
axes, and general broadcast/transpose extent ordering. Controls reject
corrupt DAGs/guards/capabilities, altered inverse/scatter comprehensions,
unused undefined helpers, mismatched supplied fields, and invalid unit-view
contracts; resource limits and backend-mode separation are checked.
The shared-IR tests additionally check identical native/symbolic construction,
retention of residual IR when bounded symbolic lowering fails, predicates
outside written guards, and paired contraction reindexing on anisotropic axes.
These development tests and inventory reruns do not constitute a new
held-out evaluation.

\paragraph{Paired specification accounting.}
All 32 cases of the earlier size protocol are attempted, including
unsuccessful pairs. Original idiomatic, expanded explicit, field-auto,
omitted-field, and compact variants have distinct recorded sources.
The primary XLA/TEPL footprint table additionally requires a reviewed
XLA branch mapping and acceptance of all required representations.
This yields 25 common cases; the larger expanded explicit/auto comparison
has 28 pairs. Expanding descriptor copies costs 323 tokens; field-auto saves 216,
omitting fields saves 224, and removing unused labels and empty blocks
saves 278. Expanding a descriptor spread is not credited as a benefit
of inference. The separate omission and cleanup steps retain the supplied
conditions and non-axis fields. Lexical tokens exclude comments and
whitespace, and the five responsibilities partition each included token
exactly once. Shared dialect and compiler costs are excluded from every
per-rule count and remain real reusable costs. The inherited invalid
negative control remains rejected.
