\begin{abstract}
Tensor rewrite rules are concise in algebraic form, but their implementations
require substantial attribute-transformation logic. Rewrite rules written as
tensor expressions describe how to change a computation, but matching alone
does not compute the attributes needed by the replacement, such as permutations
and contraction dimensions.
TEPL (Tensor Equality Pattern Language) is a domain-specific language for
describing tensor rewrites, explicit conditions, and attribute changes together.
More importantly, TEPL can automatically synthesize replacement attributes
from operation semantics. Its synthesis approach uses Axes IR and symbolic
axis mapping to handle tensors of arbitrary rank within the supported rewrite
fragment. We give a mathematical synthesis algorithm and prove constructive
completeness for a read-once fragment of contraction, permutation, and unary
summation, with a restricted additive extension. The theorem concerns this
algorithm; the current implementation covers only part of the fragment.
Our intended guarantee is that every semantically verified rewrite
has computable replacement attributes and produces a legal replacement with
equivalent results under its stated conditions and arithmetic policy. 
We evaluate TEPL on 209 XLA-derived tensor rewrite cases. TEPL supports the
operation semantics needed for 118 cases and verifies 86 with explicit
attributes. Attribute inference succeeds on all 20 verified axis/extent
cases (100\%).
\end{abstract}
\keywords{tensor rewrites, attribute synthesis, symbolic axis mapping, rewrite languages}
