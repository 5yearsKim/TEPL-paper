\begin{abstract}
TEPL synthesizes reusable axis-attribute programs for tensor rewrites in a
restricted whole-axis fragment, replacing handwritten permutation and
contraction-dimension logic. For read-once contraction, permutation,
summation, and restricted addition, we prove constructive completeness
under explicit structural and extent assumptions. A certified backend combines the constructor with sound extensions,
ensuring legal, equivalent replacements under written guards. Generated programs handle varying ranks
without repeating symbolic inference.
Default and certified backends each accept 31 of 38 XLA-derived inference
tasks. An XLA pass integration replaces handwritten attribute logic while
preserving upstream test graphs.
On 25 paired cases, inference reduces specification tokens by 17.8\%
relative to explicit TEPL. The formal guarantees apply to the stated fragments;
broader operation support is evaluated empirically.
\end{abstract}
