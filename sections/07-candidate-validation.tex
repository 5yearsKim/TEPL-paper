\section{Candidate Validation}
\label{sec:candidate-validation}
Sections~\ref{sec:mathematical-synthesis}--\ref{sec:method}
justify the canonical construction within its stated fragment.
Together with guard certification, that construction provides a
direct route to accepting a rewrite. TEPL also handles attribute
programs supplied by users, produced by other construction strategies,
or obtained through symbolic lowering. This section explains how TEPL
checks such completed candidates independently of their origin.

Validation is performed at compile time. It proves correctness for
every legal source match satisfying the written guard. At matching
time, TEPL evaluates the guard and attribute expressions; it does
not repeat the symbolic equivalence proof.
