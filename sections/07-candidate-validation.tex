\section{Candidate Validation}
\label{sec:candidate-validation}
Conditional validation establishes Section~\ref{sec:synthesis-and-execution-contract}'s execution
contract for completed attribute builders, independently of how their
attributes were obtained. It applies to inferred and user-supplied
attributes. When no generated restriction is needed, the feasibility
check always returns true.
