\section{Candidate Validation}
\label{sec:candidate-validation}
A completed RHS attribute program may be supplied by a user or produced
by a construction strategy outside the proved constructor. TEPL must
establish that the program computes legal attributes and preserves the
rewrite's result for every legal match satisfying the written guard.
Candidate validation checks this at compile time, independently of how
the attributes were generated.
