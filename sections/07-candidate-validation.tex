\section{Candidate Validation}
\label{sec:candidate-validation}
TEPL validates completed attribute programs at compile time, independently
of how their attributes were obtained. It first checks that the generated
feasibility predicate can be evaluated safely. Under the written condition
and a true feasibility result, it then establishes that the attribute
program is defined and produces a legal replacement with the same result.
This applies to inferred and user-supplied attributes; when no generated
restriction is needed, $E_q$ is true.
