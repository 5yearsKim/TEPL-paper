\subsection{Scope and Limitations}
\label{sec:scope-limitations}
Mixed explicit/inferred axis fields get soundness only, within the stated
domains of validation and the sound schemas
(Section~\ref{sec:formal-scope}).
Extent and coordinate parameters remain supplied or defaulted. Slice, padding, reshape,
and convolution are outside the direct synthesis and validation theorems;
their implementation support is limited. Tensor-valued indexing and richer
reshape, padding, and reduction semantics remain unsupported.
Appendix~\ref{app:operation-coverage}
distinguishes operation support from theorem scope. The 86 catalog cases
without an established model remain unvalidated; structural extensibility
alone supplies no semantic guarantee.

Conditional validation can remain unresolved even when a feasibility
program has been generated. Its proof search is not complete. Outside the
complete synthesis fragment, generated applicability checks may reject
feasible matches. Even within that fragment, the completeness theorem does
not cover zero or singleton extents. Successful compilation alone does not
establish that a rule has an applicable match. The independent validator
can also accept supplied attributes when inference fails. The XLA integration
covers default dense-f32 dot metadata, retaining convolution and a custom
policy by hand. Production opportunity frequency, cache hit rates, and
developer productivity remain unassessed.
