\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:scope-limitations}
TEPL automates axis transformations, but users still need to supply
or provide defaults for extent and coordinate parameters. Rewrites
that change dimension sizes or select individual coordinates therefore
require additional work. Support for slice, padding, reshape, and
convolution remains limited, and these operations fall outside the
direct synthesis and validation theorems. Tensor-valued indexing
and richer reshape, padding, and reduction semantics remain unsupported.

Outside the complete synthesis domain, TEPL may fail to infer
attributes even when a valid assignment exists. Validation may also
remain unresolved for a correct rewrite. Adding an operation declaration
does not by itself establish that TEPL can infer or validate its
attribute transformations.

TEPL accounts for floating-point behavior through a selectable
arithmetic policy. However, the completeness theorem assumes exact
arithmetic and does not cover floating-point semantics.
