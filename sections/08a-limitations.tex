\subsection{Scope and Limitations}
\label{sec:scope-limitations}
Mixed explicit/inferred axis fields have no completeness guarantee;
validation and the certified schemas provide soundness only within their
stated domains. Extent and
coordinate parameters remain supplied or defaulted. Slice, padding, reshape,
and convolution are outside the direct synthesis and validation theorems;
their implementation support is limited. Tensor-valued indexing and richer
reshape, padding, and reduction semantics remain unsupported.
Appendix~\ref{app:operation-coverage}
distinguishes operation support from theorem scope. The 86 catalog cases
without an established model remain unvalidated; structural extensibility
alone supplies no semantic guarantee.

The core certificates are sufficient checks for specified templates, and
general guard reasoning remains incomplete. The independent validator can
also accept supplied attributes when inference fails. The XLA integration
covers default dense-f32 dot metadata, retaining convolution and a custom
policy by hand. Production opportunity frequency, cache hit rates, and
developer productivity remain unassessed.
