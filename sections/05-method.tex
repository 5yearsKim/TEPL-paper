\section{Proposed Method}
\label{sec:method}
The method turns operation semantics into programs that compute missing
replacement attributes. It first expresses the source computation in Axes IR
and connects the logical axes used by its operations. It then matches source
reads with reads in the proposed replacement, determines which axes each
replacement operation must preserve, and constructs its attributes from the
recorded positions. Finally, it checks the completed rewrite.

We introduce these steps with dot distribution and then follow dot
reassociation through mapping, synthesis, and verification. Distribution
illustrates attribute reuse; reassociation requires constructing new
descriptors for a different intermediate tensor. All algebraic transformations
in these examples require the stated guards and an arithmetic policy that
permits the relevant laws.
