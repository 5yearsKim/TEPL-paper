\subsection{Core Emission Measurements}
\label{app:core-emission-results}
These supplementary results use the new core emitter, separately from the frozen corpus/verification binary. One warmup and ten repetitions per configuration give 165 calls over 15 automatic configurations. Parsing and semantic-plan preparation are outside timing; preparation explicitly defers guard proofs. Emission includes structural admission and DAG generation. Execution includes source replay, predicate evaluation, and conditional attribute construction on canonical legal metadata. Every result is compared with the separate concrete constructor and positional replay outside timing.
