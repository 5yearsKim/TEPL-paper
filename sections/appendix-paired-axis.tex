\subsection{Paired Axis-Inference and Validation Outcomes}
\label{app:paired-axis}
This experiment attempts all 38 frozen requested-inference tasks, preserving
original applicability conditions, non-axis fields and dialect declarations.
For each task, the existing field-expansion generator restores the handwritten
axis expressions and their helper dependencies to form an explicit baseline.
It checks that pattern, guard, supplied non-axis values, and adapter token
categories agree between the two variants. The broadcast/transpose/broadcast
companion has a previously frozen alternative shape expression; its explicit
baseline restores only its existing mapping helper $d$ into the automatic
\nolinkurl{dimensions} field, leaving every other byte unchanged.

The seven common failures preserve the first reported obligation in both
variants. \nolinkurl{slice_reverse}, \nolinkurl{expr_slice_reverse_general}, and
\nolinkurl{catalog_broadcast_slice_broadcast_only} stop at metadata definedness.
\nolinkurl{expr_convolution_scale_filter_bias},
\nolinkurl{catalog_broadcast_slice_broadcast}, and
\nolinkurl{catalog_dot_slice_output} stop at replacement-legality obligations.
\nolinkurl{catalog_broadcast_divide_broadcast} stops at unequal normalized
coefficients, reads or reduction domains. The three inference-only successes
are the broadcast/transpose, broadcast/transpose/broadcast companion, and
reverse/inserted-unit-dimension cases. Their supplied expressions remain
unresolved; the comparison does not establish that those expressions are
mathematically incorrect.

Both variants use one optimized compiler, the algebraic policy, and a
30-second limit per command. No timing conclusion is drawn, and no guard
is repaired after observing failure. Raw sources, all outcomes and
diagnostics, generated programs, hashes and the runner are retained in
\nolinkurl{evaluation/paired-axis/}. This comparison distinguishes failures of
the full automation workflow from failures unique to inference. It neither
removes the seven failures from the denominator nor enlarges semantic
modeling coverage beyond the 123 frozen cases.
